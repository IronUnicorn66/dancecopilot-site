扒舞搭子 / DanceCopilot

离线优先的练舞工具

中文

扒舞搭子帮助你在自己的手机上拆解、跟练和记录舞蹈。视频处理、节拍分析和练习记录默认留在本机，不提供注册账号，也不收集用于广告或分析的遥测数据。

会员购买与恢复购买由 Apple 处理。如需帮助，请查看支持页；数据处理说明见隐私政策。

English

DanceCopilot is an offline-first dance practice app. Video processing, beat analysis, and practice records stay on your device. There is no account system and no advertising or analytics telemetry.

Apple handles membership purchases and restorations. See the support page for contact details and the privacy policy for data practices.
