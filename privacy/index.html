扒舞搭子 / DanceCopilot

隐私政策 · Privacy Policy · 生效日期 2026-09-16

中文

扒舞搭子（DanceCopilot）是一款离线优先的练舞应用。我们不提供注册账号，不运营用户云端资料库，也不收集用于广告或分析的遥测数据。

本应用处理的信息如下：

  • 视频、节拍分析、人物跟踪、练习记录和项目文件均在你的设备本地处理与保存。
  • 首次打开时赠送的高级体验记录保存在本机 Keychain，不随应用数据迁移，也不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 会员购买、恢复购买、退款和订阅管理由 Apple 处理。我们不会收到你的银行卡号，也不会自行扣费。
  • 系统设备备份是否包含应用数据，由你的 iOS 设置决定。

我们不会把上述信息出售给第三方，也不会将其用于跨 App 追踪。如需管理或取消自动续期订阅，请使用 Apple 的订阅设置。

如对隐私政策有疑问，请发邮件至 ironunicorn@163.com，也可通过应用内「反馈与联系」联系开发者。

English

DanceCopilot is an offline-first practice app. It has no account system, no cloud user database, and no advertising or analytics telemetry.

Information handled by the app:

  • Videos, beat analysis, person tracking, practice records, and project files are processed and stored on your device.
  • The complimentary Premium trial record is stored in the on-device Keychain. It is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Membership purchases, restorations, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details and do not charge you ourselves.
  • Whether system backups include app data follows your iOS settings.

We do not sell this information or use it for tracking across apps. To manage or cancel an auto-renewing subscription, use Apple Subscription settings.

Questions about this policy can be sent to ironunicorn@163.com or through the in-app Feedback & contact details.
