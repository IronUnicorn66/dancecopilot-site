DanceCopilot · Effective date 16 September 2026

扒舞搭子（DanceCopilot）是一款离线优先的练舞应用。我们不提供注册账号，不运营用户云端资料库，也不收集用于广告或分析的遥测数据。

本应用处理的信息

  • 视频、节拍分析、人物跟踪、练习记录和项目文件均在你的设备本地处理与保存。
  • 最多 5 个本机练习项目的高级功能试用记在这台 iPhone 的 Keychain，不随卸载删除、不同步 iCloud、不绑定我们的账号（本应用无注册）。抹掉所有内容和设置或换设备后可能重新获得试用。该记录不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 终身会员的购买、恢复购买和退款由 Apple 处理。我们不会收到你的银行卡号，也不会自行扣费。
  • 系统设备备份是否包含应用数据，由你的 iOS 设置决定。

我们不会做什么

我们不会把上述信息出售给第三方，也不会将其用于跨 App 追踪。终身会员是一次性非消耗型购买，不自动续费。

如何联系

如对隐私政策有疑问，请发邮件至 ironunicorn@163.com，也可通过应用内「反馈与联系」联系开发者。

DanceCopilot is an offline-first practice app. It has no account system, no cloud user database, and no advertising or analytics telemetry.

Information handled by the app

  • Videos, beat analysis, person tracking, practice records, and project files are processed and stored on your device.
  • Up to 5 on-device practice projects can use Premium tools for free. That quota is stored in this iPhone's Keychain. It survives uninstall, is not synced with iCloud, and is not tied to an account in this app (there is no registration). Erasing all content and settings or moving to a new device may grant a new trial. The record is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Lifetime membership purchases, restorations, and refunds are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details and do not charge you ourselves.
  • Whether system backups include app data follows your iOS settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell this information or use it for tracking across apps. Lifetime membership is a one-time non-consumable purchase with no auto-renewal.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ironunicorn@163.com or through the in-app Feedback & contact details.
