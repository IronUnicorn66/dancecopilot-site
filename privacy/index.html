扒舞搭子（DanceCopilot）是一款离线优先的舞蹈练习应用。iOS 试用无需登录，点数购买、活动和邀请奖励使用可选的 Apple 登录账号。不运营云端练习资料库，也不收集用于广告或分析的遥测数据。本政策说明你使用本应用时，我们如何处理与练习功能有关的信息。

请在使用前阅读本政策。如果不同意，请停止使用本应用。

一、应用基本信息

应用名称：扒舞搭子（DanceCopilot）

开发者联系邮箱：ironunicorn@163.com

扒舞搭子用于在本机完成舞蹈练习，主要包括：从相册或文件导入视频、本机节拍分析、变速与镜像、逐帧笔记、电子数拍、人物跟踪直拍、双视频对齐与合成导出、前置镜头跟练镜子、跟练录制，以及本机练习记录和个人资料。

二、我们处理的信息

1. 你导入的视频和音频

当你从系统相册或文件选择器导入视频时，应用会在本机处理你主动选择的文件，用于播放、节拍分析、练习、笔记、直拍、对比和导出。当前版本不会把导入的视频上传到开发者服务器，也不会将其用于广告、用户画像或其他与练舞无关的用途。

2. 节拍分析与电子数拍

使用节拍分析或电子数拍时，应用会在本机处理导入视频中的音频，用于识别节拍并生成数拍。分析在设备本地完成。音频不会被用于建立兴趣画像，也不会出售或提供给广告商。

3. 人物跟踪直拍

生成直拍时，应用会在本机分析画面中的人物位置，用于生成跟踪人物的练习视频。原始视频和直拍结果保存在本机；只有你选择保存时，才会写入系统相册。我们不会把这些视频用于广告，或用于与本应用无关的模型训练。

4. 相机和麦克风

跟练镜子使用前置镜头在本机显示画面，不会因此上传视频。只有你主动拍摄时，应用才会录制练习视频。麦克风仅用于拍摄时录入现场音乐和动作声音。这些权限只在你进入相应功能并授权后使用。

录制文件会先保存在本机。你选择保留或保存到相册后，应用会按本机文件管理处理临时文件。我们不会把录制视频上传到开发者服务器。

5. 本地视频、笔记、练习记录和个人资料

应用可能在设备本地保存：

  • 视频名称和文件
  • 节拍分析结果
  • 播放、镜像和其他练习设置
  • 逐帧笔记
  • 双视频对齐与对比设置
  • 练习记录
  • 你填写的昵称、简介和头像
  • 语言偏好

这些信息用于重新打开视频、恢复练习状态，以及在「我的」中展示本机资料。它们仅用于本机展示和功能恢复，不会上传，也不会用于建立开发者侧用户档案。

6. 试用和会员权益

Android 买断授权需要购买邮箱、邮箱验证码和分配给该邮箱的兑换码；服务端保存新生成兑换码的完整文本及摘要、邮箱、订单引用、兑换状态、激活时间及设备绑定，用于订单交付、核验购买、同机恢复和加密备份。完整兑换码不写入普通日志或可读备份表格。应用使用 Android ID、应用标识和应用签名生成设备摘要，不上传原始 Android ID，不读取 IMEI 或手机号码。设备私钥由 Android Keystore 保护，不上传。

永久会员绑定一台设备，首版不提供换机或解绑。同机重装可验证原购买邮箱恢复；恢复出厂设置或设备标识变化可能无法恢复。领取和激活后的本地验签、计次及媒体处理可离线进行，启动或回到前台不会自动检查授权服务器。你可以不领取、不兑换，继续使用内置示例及已保存内容。

六、信息的保存和删除

1. 保存位置

视频、笔记、练习记录、个人资料和设置保存在你的设备本地，直至你删除相应视频或内容，或卸载本应用。高级功能试用名额保存在这台 iPhone 的 Keychain，是卸载后仍可能保留的例外。已经保存到系统相册的视频，由你在「照片」中管理。系统设备备份是否包含应用数据，由你的 iOS 设置决定。Android 授权凭证与加密试用记录保存在禁止系统备份及设备迁移的应用私有目录；卸载会删除本地凭证，但不会删除服务器上的领取与授权记录。

2. 删除方式

你可以通过以下方式删除相关数据：

  • 在应用内删除单个视频
  • 在「存储」页清理可再生成的缓存；清理不会删除视频、笔记、录制、个人资料或试用名单
  • 在「照片」中管理已经保存到相册的视频
  • 删除本应用，以清除应用沙盒中的本地数据

卸载后，Keychain 中的试用记录通常仍会保留。相册里的视频不会随卸载自动删除。在应用内删除视频不会删除相册中的原件。

如果仍有与你相关的数据需要处理，可通过本政策末尾的邮箱联系我们。

如果你主动发送邮件，发件地址和邮件内容仅在处理咨询、解决争议、履行必要法律义务或合理维护支持记录所需期间保留；你可以通过同一邮箱请求删除。

Android 试用领取和永久授权记录在提供长期授权、防止重复领取、处理订单或争议所需期间保留。验证码与请求凭证到期后自动失效；短期限流与缓存记录按最多约两天的窗口，在后续请求时清理；服务不保存明文验证码，IP 限流键使用不可直接还原的 HMAC 摘要。云平台的安全日志按实际服务配置保留。你可通过联系邮箱请求查询、更正或删除授权信息；处理前需核验购买归属，并说明删除对恢复授权的影响。

1. About DanceCopilot

App name: DanceCopilot (扒舞搭子)

Developer contact: ironunicorn@163.com

DanceCopilot helps you practice on your device. Its features include importing videos from Photos or Files, on-device beat analysis, speed and mirroring, frame notes, electronic counting, person-tracked direct-shot video, two-video alignment and export, a front-camera practice mirror, practice recording, and on-device practice records and profile details.

2. Information we process

2.1 Videos and audio you import

When you import a video from Photos or the file picker, the app processes the file you selected on your device for playback, beat analysis, practice, notes, direct shot, comparison, and export. The current version does not upload imported videos to a developer-operated server, and does not use them for advertising, profiling, or purposes unrelated to dance practice.

2.2 Beat analysis and electronic counting

When you use beat analysis or electronic counting, the app processes audio from the imported video on your device to identify beats and generate counting. This analysis stays on the device. Audio is not used to build an interest profile and is not sold or provided to advertisers.

2.3 Person-tracked direct shot

When you generate a direct-shot video, the app analyzes person positions on your device to produce a practice clip that follows the dancer. Source videos and results stay on the device; they are written to Photos only if you choose to save them. We do not use these videos for advertising or to train models unrelated to this app.

2.4 Camera and microphone

The practice mirror uses the front camera to show a local preview and does not upload that video. The app records only when you start filming. The microphone is used only to capture live music and movement sound while recording. These permissions are used only after you enter the relevant feature and grant access.

Recordings are stored on your device first. After you keep a clip or save it to Photos, temporary files are handled by the app’s local file management. We do not upload recorded videos to a developer-operated server.

2.5 Local videos, notes, practice records, and profile

The app may store the following on your device:

  • Video names and files
  • Beat-analysis results
  • Playback, mirror, and other practice settings
  • Frame notes
  • Two-video alignment and comparison settings
  • Practice records
  • The nickname, bio, and avatar you enter
  • Language preference

This information is used to reopen videos, restore practice state, and show your on-device profile. It is used only for on-device display and feature restoration, is not uploaded, and is not used to build a developer-side user profile.

2.6 Trial and membership

New iOS users receive 3 credits; existing users keep their remaining allowance. One credit unlocks one external material, with no extra charge for reuse. Trial state is protected by the device Keychain, is not synced through iCloud, and is not refunded by deleting material or reinstalling. When you choose Sign in with Apple, the remaining trial and irreversible fingerprints of unlocked materials are linked to your account. A second device does not add another base allowance. Account credits are shared across devices; new unlocks require internet and unlocked materials work offline. Videos, audio, filenames and notes are not uploaded.

iOS lifetime membership is a one-time purchase with no auto-renewal. Purchases, restorations, and refunds are handled by Apple. We only read Apple-provided and verified product, transaction, and entitlement status needed to unlock features. We do not receive your payment card details and do not charge you ourselves.

2.6.1 Android trial and activation

The Android APK contacts the licensing service only when you choose to claim a trial, request an email code, redeem membership, or restore a license. Claiming sends the application identifier, a device-binding digest, device public key, and signed proof. The server records one trial, normally 10 successful external video imports. Usage and imported file lists stay on your device. Clearing app data or reinstalling does not grant another trial.

Android activation requires the purchase email, an email verification code, and an activation code assigned to that email. The server keeps the complete text and digest of newly generated activation codes, email, order reference, redemption status, activation time, and device binding for order delivery, purchase verification, same-device restoration, and encrypted backups. Complete activation codes are excluded from ordinary logs and readable backup tables. The app derives a digest from Android ID, application ID, and the app signature; it does not send the raw Android ID or access IMEI or phone numbers. The device private key stays in Android Keystore.

Lifetime membership is bound to one device, with no transfers or unbinding in this version. Reinstalling on the same device allows restoration with the original purchase email; a factory reset or changed device identifier may prevent restoration. Once claimed or activated, verification, trial counting, and media processing work offline. Startup and resume do not contact the licensing service. You may choose not to activate and continue using built-in examples and saved content.

2.6.2 iOS update checks

When you open App Settings, the iOS app automatically asks Apple for the public store version and release notes. You can also tap Check for Updates to check again. The query contains only the public App Store app identifier and store region; it does not send videos, practice data, membership records, or device identifiers. Apple receives connection information such as your IP address needed to handle the request. Results are cached briefly in memory while the app is running. A failed update check does not affect offline practice.

2.7 Email you send us

The iOS app does not require an email address. Android trial claims do not require one, but redemption and restoration require the purchase email. The app does not include an uploaded feedback form. In-app Feedback & contact only shows the developer email address for you to copy and use in an external email client.
