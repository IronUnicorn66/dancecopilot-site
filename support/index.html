点数与邀请（测试版）

Credits & invitations (beta)

以下规则适用于最新 TestFlight 点数测试版。测试余额不会带入正式版。

These rules apply to the latest TestFlight beta with credits enabled. Test balances do not transfer to the public release.

新用户与素材解锁

New users and material unlocks

首次启动 App 赠送 3 点，无需登录即可使用；老用户保留原有剩余额度。1 点解锁 1 份外部素材，重复练习和导出不再扣点。双视频对比左侧是要学习的参考舞蹈视频，右侧是自己的练习视频；两侧素材分别判断是否需要解锁。

New users receive 3 credits on first launch, usable without signing in. Existing users keep their remaining balance. One credit unlocks one external material; repeat practice and exports use no extra credits. In two-video comparison, the reference dance is on the left and your practice video is on the right. Each material is checked separately for unlocking.

邀请与活动

Invitations and campaigns

首次通过邀请链接联网打开 App，登录后自动领取 2 点，邀请人也获得 2 点。受邀奖励每个账号限领一次；邀请人每个月最多获得 10 点，达到上限不影响好友领取。新用户赠送与邀请奖励独立，领取邀请奖励无需导入素材，也不扣点。通过活动链接领取赠送点数，具体以活动说明为准。

Open your first invitation link in the app while online and sign in to automatically receive 2 credits. The inviter also receives 2 credits. Each account can receive an invitation reward once. Inviters earn up to 10 credits per month; reaching the limit does not affect the friend’s reward. New-user credits and invitation rewards are separate. No material import or credit spending is required to claim. Claim campaign credits through a campaign link; see the campaign details for its terms.

登录、联网与点数记录

Sign-in, connectivity and credit history

购买点数、领取邀请或活动奖励、恢复账号余额需要 Apple 登录和联网。绑定点数账号后，解锁新素材需要联网；已解锁素材可离线使用。点数永久有效。记录按时间显示“增加点数”和“使用点数”；增加记录保留来源，成功扣点才生成使用记录。迁移前只有剩余额度时显示为“继承试用点数”，不补造历史消费记录。

Buying credits, claiming invitation or campaign rewards, and restoring an account balance require Sign in with Apple and an internet connection. After linking a credit account, unlocking new materials requires internet; unlocked materials work offline. Credits never expire. History shows Credits added and Credits used by date, with sources for additions and a separate entry for each successful debit. A migrated remaining balance is shown as Inherited trial credits, without inventing past spending records.

领取与到账问题

Claim and balance issues

安装后邀请未识别：重新打开原链接，登录后自动领取；取消登录不会发奖，联网失败可重试，重复打开不会重复发奖。已付款未到账：使用原 Apple 账号登录，在“点数与高级功能”中联网打开“点数补发”，不要重复购买。退款仅撤回对应购买的未使用点数，已解锁素材保留，购买记录保留退款状态。

If an invitation is missed after installation, reopen the original link and sign in to claim automatically. Canceling sign-in grants no reward; retry after a connection failure. Reopening a link does not grant duplicate rewards. Paid but not credited: sign in with your original Apple account and open Missing credits in Credits & advanced features while online. Do not purchase again. Refunds revoke unused credits from that purchase, keep unlocked materials, and remain marked on the purchase record.

Service information

Use materials you have the right to import, practice with and share. Videos, audio and practice notes are processed on device. Credit accounts process the data needed for balances, transactions, rewards and unlocks. Lifetime Membership and credits are separate purchases and cannot be offset against or exchanged for each other through refunds. Prices and available features are shown in the app.

The iOS app uses Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement. See the Privacy Policy for data handling, account deletion and contact information.
